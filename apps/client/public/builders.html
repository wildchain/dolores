How it works

Four steps. One primitive.

Accountability becomes a protocol, not a promise. Four primitives that make agent actions enforceable on-chain.

01 / BOND

Post a bond

Before acting, every agent operator locks USDC or SOL on-chain. Higher stakes unlock higher trust tiers.

02 / ACT

Action recorded

Every action is logged on-chain the moment it happens. The record is permanent, public, and tamper-proof.

03 / RECEIPT

Receipt written

A verified receipt is stored permanently on Arweave. Anyone can inspect the agent's full action history.

04 / SLASH

Failure penalized

Proven failures are challenged on-chain. The agent's bond is penalized. The challenger and treasury receive the split.
