Section 06

Staking and Slashing Risk

THIS SECTION CONTAINS THE MOST IMPORTANT RISK DISCLOSURES IN THESE TERMS. READ IT IN FULL BEFORE STAKING ANY TOKENS.

6.1 What You Can Lose — All Currencies

The Dolores Protocol accepts staking in three currencies: $DOL tokens, SOL, and USDC. All three are subject to the same slashing mechanism. SOL and USDC staked as registration deposits are equally subject to permanent seizure in a slash event — the slashing risk is not limited to $DOL holders.

When a slash fires, the Protocol seizes the Operator's staked deposit regardless of which currency was used. 60% goes to the Challenger, 40% to the Protocol treasury. Dolores has no ability to recover, return, or compensate you for any of these tokens in any currency.

6.2 Slashing — Permanent and Irreversible

When a slash fires, your staked tokens are gone. Permanently. Dolores cannot return slashed tokens. There is no insurance fund, no appeal to Dolores, and no legal or arbitral mechanism that can compel Dolores to compensate you for a correctly executed slash — in any currency.

6.3 Slash Records — Permanence and Arweave Storage

Confirmed slash records and attestations are stored on the Solana blockchain and may also be anchored to Arweave for permanent, immutable archival. Arweave is a decentralised storage network outside Dolores's control that is, by design, uncensorable and permanent. Records anchored to Arweave cannot be deleted by Dolores, the Operator, or any other party, and are permanently accessible to any party with the relevant transaction ID.

Each confirmed slash also applies a compounding score multiplier of ×0.35 per event. Two slashes reduce a previously high trust score to approximately 12% of its prior value. This penalty cannot be erased or bought off.

6.4 Provisional Flag and Stake Hold

Upon a reported failure, a provisional score penalty of 25% of the full slash amount is applied immediately and is visible to all protocols querying your Agent. Your stake enters a dispute hold — withdrawals are blocked. You cannot unstake to avoid a slash while a dispute is active. This provisional penalty may affect your Agent's access to integrating protocols during the window even if the challenge ultimately fails.

6.5 Challenges by Third Parties

Any person who has posted a Challenger bond may file a slash claim against your Agent. The Protocol processes challenges without requiring your pre-approval. Challengers who file incorrect claims lose their bond, which deters frivolous challenges — but Dolores cannot guarantee frivolous challenges will never be filed.

6.6 Acknowledgement of Risk

By staking tokens with the Protocol, you expressly acknowledge and accept that:

  • You may lose all staked tokens ($DOL, SOL, or USDC) in a slash event.
  • Loss from slashing is not recoverable from Dolores, the Protocol treasury, or any other party.
  • The risk of slashing is a fundamental feature of the Protocol, not a bug or unintended consequence.
  • You have independently evaluated whether staking is appropriate for your financial situation.
  • Dolores has not provided financial, investment, or legal advice in connection with your decision to stake.

Section 07

The Adjudication System

The Protocol uses a two-stage adjudication system that separates the immediate protocol response from fault attribution. It is designed to deter bad actors while protecting Operators from being penalised for genuine infrastructure failures outside their control.

7.1 Stage 1 — Provisional Flag (Automatic)

Stage 1 fires automatically when a failure is reported. No human is involved. A provisional score penalty of 25% of the full slash amount is applied immediately. The Operator's stake enters a dispute hold — withdrawals are blocked. A 72-hour dispute window opens for the Operator to submit evidence. If no evidence is submitted, a full slash fires automatically at the window's close.

7.2 Stage 2 — Resolution (Three Paths)

Path A — Exoneration

Infrastructure Exoneration

Operator submits valid infrastructure proof. Oracle confirms a Solana network degradation event overlapped the failure window. Slash voided automatically. Provisional hit reversed. Stake released. Recorded as infrastructure event.

Path B — Auto-Slash

Timeout Auto-Slash

No evidence submitted within 72 hours. Full slash fires automatically. Stake seized 60% to Challenger, 40% to treasury. Permanent slash record written on-chain. No further appeal available.

Path C — Escalation

Contested Failure

Evidence submitted but oracle inconclusive. Case escalates to expert panel. Operator posts additional bond (larger than the challenger bond) to proceed. DAO is the last-resort appeal body.

7.3 The Force Majeure Oracle

The oracle queries on-chain data produced by Solana's own infrastructure. It checks whether any of the following conditions were confirmed at the failure timestamp:

  • Solana network-wide outage or degraded slot confirmation rates (>50% of slots missing for >5 minutes)
  • Confirmed RPC provider degradation affecting >30% of endpoint availability
  • Network congestion causing >10× normal transaction confirmation latency at the relevant block height

Oracle data is sourced from a decentralised network status feed anchored on-chain — not any Dolores-controlled source. Neither the Operator nor Dolores can influence oracle results. Dolores does not warrant that the oracle will be available at all times or that its determinations will be free from error.

7.4 Expert Panel and DAO

Path C disputes escalate to a specialised expert panel. If the panel's verdict is disputed, the DAO serves as last-resort appeal body. DAO governance parameters are subject to change following activation at 50,000 registered agents. Expert panel and DAO decisions are binding. Dolores has no power to override them.

Section 08

Delegated Trust Staking

8.1 Overview

$DOL holders may delegate stake behind registered Agents at Developing tier (Trust Score ≥ 400) or above as a public signal of trust. Delegated staking is not a passive investment product. It is an active economic participation mechanism in the Protocol's accountability system. SOL and USDC operators cannot participate as delegators — delegation is a $DOL-native benefit only.

8.2 Delegation Rewards

Delegators receive protocol rewards from the 400M registry rewards pool, scaled by the trust tier of the Agent they back. Rewards are expressed in $DOL tokens. Their USD value depends on $DOL market price, which is not guaranteed and may be zero. No specific return, APY, or yield is promised by Dolores. Rewards are protocol participation incentives, not investment returns.

8.3 Delegator Slash Exposure

If the Agent you delegate to is slashed, you will automatically lose 15% of your delegated stake. This loss is proportional to your delegation size and is automatic and on-chain. You cannot opt out of slash exposure while delegated. Slash exposure persists during the 10-day unbonding period — you cannot unstake to avoid a pending slash.

8.4 Unbonding Period

Delegated stake is subject to a 10-day unbonding period on withdrawal. During unbonding, your stake earns no rewards but remains exposed to slash events arising from disputes active at the time of your unbonding request.

8.5 Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for evaluating the Agents you delegate to. Dolores does not endorse any Agent, does not guarantee any Agent's trust score will be maintained, and has no liability for losses arising from your delegation choices.
