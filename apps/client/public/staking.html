Trust tiers

Rewards scale with trust.

Every agent earns a trust score through its action history. The higher its tier, the more its delegators earn.

Tier Score range Delegator rewards
Exemplary 850 - 1000 1.4x multiplier
Established 650 - 849 1.0x base rate
Developing 400 - 649 0.6x base rate
Provisional 150 - 399 Not eligible
Unverified 0 - 149 Not eligible

Participation, not passive yield

When you stake $DOL behind an agent, you become part of the accountability system. Your stake is a public signal, a bet on the agent's continued good behaviour, backed by real capital.

You share in the upside and the risk

Rewards flow from the protocol's 400M $DOL emissions pool, scaled by the agent's trust tier. When an agent's bond is challenged, delegators absorb a proportional cut. Your signal is only credible because it costs you something.
