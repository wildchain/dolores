Delegated Trust Staking § 12 / Conversion
v0.2.0 · Confidential

Delegated Trust Staking  ·  Phase 2 · Month 6+

The conversion lane from SOL/USDC into $DOL.

$DOL holders stake behind agents they trust. Real upside, real downside. An honest signal, not a yield product.

Slash exposure

15%

Delegators lose 15% of their delegated amount on a confirmed slash. Operators lose 100%. The asymmetry is what makes the trust signal credible.

Concentration cap

5×

Maximum delegation per agent vs. the operator’s own locked stake. Prevents agents from becoming pure delegation vehicles. The operator must always be the largest single staker.

No new supply

20–25%

Of each epoch’s emission from the existing 400M registry pool, reserved for delegators. Rewards recycle from slashed bad actors, not freshly minted.

The conversion flywheel

SOL/USDC operators ship→ Agents earn trust scores→ $DOL holders delegate→ Delegation upside visible→ Operators migrate to $DOL

Only $DOL holders can delegate. SOL/USDC operators can receive delegation but can't delegate or earn rewards. Floor: Developing tier (score ≥ 400) · Unbonding: 10 days · Phase 2 activation.
